Vehicles

Modern cars are computers on wheels. The technology inside now matters as much as the engine.

What makes a car smart

Today's vehicles combine big touchscreens, connected navigation, phone integration and driver-assistance systems. Features like adaptive cruise control, lane keeping and automatic emergency braking help the driver stay safe, though they assist rather than replace the driver.

Many cars can also receive software updates over the air, so features and fixes arrive without a visit to a dealership.

How vehicle technology advanced

  1. 1980sElectronic engine control. Computers began managing fuel and ignition for better efficiency.
  2. 1990s to 2000sSafety electronics. Anti-lock brakes, airbags and stability control became widespread.
  3. 2010sScreens and assistance. Touchscreen infotainment, parking sensors and cameras, and early driver-assistance features arrived.
  4. TodayElectric and connected. Electric and hybrid cars, over-the-air updates and advanced driver assistance are increasingly common.

Electric and hybrid in the islands

Electric cars are quiet, quick and cheap to run, but they depend on charging. Before choosing one, check where you can charge at home and around your island. Hybrids are an easier step where public charging is still limited.

What I look for

  • Safety features: automatic emergency braking and good visibility aids.
  • Infotainment: a fast, simple screen with phone connection.
  • Software updates: whether the maker supports over-the-air updates.
  • Efficiency or range: running costs and, for electric, realistic range.
  • Local support: service and parts available where you live.
