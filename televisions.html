Smart Devices / Televisions

Televisions

Modern TVs are smart devices with built-in apps, voice control and screens far sharper than the sets of a few decades ago.

What matters in a TV

Picture quality comes down to the panel type, brightness and contrast. Smart features matter too, because a TV with a slow, cluttered menu is frustrating even if the picture is excellent.

In a bright, sunny room, brightness is especially important, since a dim screen looks washed out in daylight.

How TV screens evolved

  1. Before 2000CRT. Bulky tube televisions were the standard for decades.
  2. 2000sFlat screens. Plasma and LCD screens made TVs thin and large enough to hang on a wall.
  3. 2010sLED backlighting and 4K. LED-lit LCD screens became affordable, and 4K resolution became common.
  4. TodayOLED and Mini-LED. OLED gives perfect blacks, while Mini-LED gives very high brightness. HDR improves colour and contrast.

Choosing a screen type

OLED TVs have excellent contrast and are great in darker rooms. LED and Mini-LED TVs usually get brighter, which suits sunny living rooms. Size should match your viewing distance, and bigger is not always better if you sit close.

What I look for

  • Panel type: OLED for contrast, LED or Mini-LED for brightness.
  • Smart platform: fast, simple menus and the streaming apps you use.
  • Ports: several HDMI inputs, ideally with HDMI 2.1 for gaming.
  • Refresh rate: 120 Hz gives smoother motion for games and sports.
  • Sound: built-in speakers are usually weak, so budget for a soundbar.
