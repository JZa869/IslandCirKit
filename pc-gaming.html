Gaming / PC

PC

A gaming PC offers the most power and freedom. You can upgrade parts, tweak every setting and play with a mouse and keyboard.

What is inside a gaming PC

The graphics card (GPU) matters most for gaming, followed by the processor (CPU), memory (RAM) and fast storage. A reliable power supply and good cooling keep everything stable.

You can buy a prebuilt PC for convenience or build one yourself for more control over cost and parts. A gaming laptop is an option if you need portability, though it costs more for the same power.

Why choose PC

PC gaming has the widest game library, the best graphics options, mods and the freedom to upgrade over time. It also doubles as a computer for work, creating and learning.

The trade-offs are a higher starting cost and more setup. Prices and availability of parts change often, so compare current options before you buy.

What I look for

  • Graphics card: the biggest factor in game performance.
  • Balanced parts: no single weak part holding the others back.
  • Storage: a fast SSD keeps loading quick.
  • Cooling and power: quiet, stable and safe.
  • Display and peripherals: a good monitor, mouse and keyboard make a big difference.
