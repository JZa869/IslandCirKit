Gaming / Mobile

Mobile

Mobile is the most accessible way to play, because you already own the device. Modern phones can run impressive games.

From Snake to console-style games

Mobile gaming began with simple games like Snake on early phones. App stores in 2008 changed everything, giving developers an easy way to reach players, and many mobile games are free to download and earn money through in-game purchases or ads.

Today's phones and tablets can run detailed 3D games, and some offer console-style experiences.

Getting the best experience

A phone with a fast chip, a smooth high-refresh screen and good cooling will handle games better. Long sessions can make a phone hot and drain the battery, so a phone with strong battery life helps.

Bluetooth controllers and clip-on grips can turn a phone into a handheld console. Be mindful of in-game spending, especially for younger players.

What I look for

  • Chip performance: a fast processor for demanding games.
  • Screen: a smooth, responsive display.
  • Battery and cooling: long sessions without overheating.
  • Controller support: play with a proper gamepad when you want.
  • Storage: games can be several gigabytes each.
